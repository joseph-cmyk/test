## :sparkle: What it does

- :palette: Warm Gruvbox dark surfaces (`#282828`, `#3c3836`, `#1d2021`) with golden-yellow accents (`#d79921`, `#fabd2f`) and cream text (`#fbf1c7`).
- :brush: Recolors chat, server and channel lists, sheets, modals, buttons, switches, mentions, reactions, and status indicators.
- :heart: Ported from Taki_Shiwa's Vendetta `GruvYellow` (v3.3.4) to Unbound's `manifest.json` + `bundle.json` format and current Discord color tokens.
